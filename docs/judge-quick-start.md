# Judge quick start — under 60 seconds

1. Open the public demo URL.
2. Click **Run demo · connect MCP** to initialize MCP `2025-11-25` and discover the tool.
3. Click **Prepare + verify** to see evidence `PASS` and trust `ACCEPT`.
4. Review the provider, service, time, price, currency, and location. Click **Approve exact action**.
5. Click **Mutate price**. The `$89 → $129` change is shown and execution is blocked.
6. Click **Execute + inject crash**. One local simulated hold is created before the controlled crash.
7. Click **Replay safely**. Confirm `DUPLICATE SUPPRESSED` and **1 simulated effect**.
8. Click **View trace** to inspect the result, reconciliation, and DecisionReceipt.
9. Optionally cancel the hold, then click **Reset demo**.

This is an Alexa+-style simulated experience for the Amazon Developer Hackathon. It performs no real booking or payment.
